Frequently asked questions

Why eir broadband?

  • eir is Ireland’s leading telecommunications provider, and Ireland No.1 broadband provider. You can enjoy 5Gb broadband speeds with eir, offering 50 times the speed of standard broadband. Unlike cable options, eir provides a dedicated broadband line straight to your home, ensuring seamless streaming with no buffering. Visit this page regularly to stay updated with our latest broadband deals, including our 5Gb fibre, powered by WiFi 7 technology.

Is fibre broadband better than standard broadband?

  • There are two types of broadband in Ireland; 100% fibre broadband (FTTH) and standard broadband (FTTC). FTTH is our strongest, fastest and most reliable broadband yet. Unlike standard broadband which can experience speed limitations and signal degradation, FTTH technology delivers your own broadband line straight to your home. This translates to 50 times faster broadband speeds and unparalleled reliability. As broadband usage continues to evolve, FTTH is built to handle these growing demands, future-proofing your connectivity. Explore the latest FTTH broadband deals from eir with Ireland’s No.1 broadband provider.

Can I get broadband without a landline in Ireland?

  • All eir broadband plans now come with landline included, at no extra cost. Landline services use VoIP (Voice over IP) technology, which provide high definition voice quality. Depending on your usage, additional add-ons are available, which is ideal for customers who make most of their calls during the day.

How much broadband speed do I need?

  • Your broadband speed, measured in megabits per second (Mb), has two key components: download speed and upload speed. A faster download speed means you can enjoy movies and games without interruptions, whereas stronger upload speed allows you to share files and videos quickly. Finding the right broadband deal in Ireland depends on your internet habits. For uninterrupted working from home, and multiple users streaming at once our 500Mb plan will suffice. Households with a desire for seamless gaming in Ultra HD and 4K should consider our 5Gb plan, which is powered by WiFi 7 technology.

Is 500Mb good for gaming and streaming?

  • Yes, you will be able to game online with a 500Mb broadband connection, but 5Gb, powered by WiFi 7 technology, will allow your household to stream 4K content, work from home and take part in online gaming without buffering. Download large files quickly, enjoy high-quality video calls without interruptions, and enjoy seamless operation of multiple smart home devices. Check out our gaming page for more details.

Is there a big difference between 500Mb and 1Gb?

  • 1Gb is equivalent to 1000Mb, which means it is an ultra-fast connection that offers more bandwidth than 500Mb. A 500Mb broadband connection is ok for online gaming and remote working, but 1Gb broadband will offer greater bandwidth for gaming, streaming 4K video and transferring large data files as it allows for faster downloads. Choose the right broadband deal depending on your requirement, and assess how much speed is important for your household.

Is there a big difference between 1Gb and 5Gb?

  • A 5Gb fibre broadband connection offers speeds of up to 5000Mb per second, which is five times greater than those offered with a 1Gb connection and 50 times faster than standard broadband. With 5Gb fibre broadband users can enjoy seamless streaming and downloads in seconds, while lower latency means the ultimate gaming experience across a range of devices at the same time.
